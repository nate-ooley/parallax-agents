A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Clear from
the beginning.

What does “plus usage” mean?

Running agents can incur AI, messaging, voice, and platform costs depending on your configuration. We confirm the channels, rates, billing method, and budget before activation. The demo’s usage figures are illustrative estimates, not a quote.

Is the city showing a real company?

No. Harbor Home Services and its activity are sample data. The demo executes a simulated workflow in your browser. A production workspace needs authenticated integrations and verified event reporting before it can display your real activity.

Can I use my own branding?

Yes—the workspace design supports a business name and brand colors. Try “Preview your brand” above. Production white-label domains, customer accounts, logos, and isolated data are configured as part of a separately scoped rollout.

What happens when an agent needs help?

We define the permitted actions and escalation owner during setup. The demo shows a failed connection and a request awaiting review. In production, retries and human approval rules must be configured and tested for your connected systems.

Can we add more agents later?

Yes. Inbox assistance, content operations, reporting, and other roles can be scoped after the starter workflow is established. Those roles and ongoing management are not included in the $999 starter setup.
